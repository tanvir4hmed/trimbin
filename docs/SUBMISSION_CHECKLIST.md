## Items to finish before submitting

- Record a public English YouTube demo of three minutes or less; the planned target is 2:45. Show actual application modules, real NVIDIA execution, editor approval and output. Do not present edited waiting time as a speed benchmark. [Overview](https://nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/).
- Use cleared source footage and verify model/media/codec rights. Generated engineering footage does not prove paid-model quality improvement.
- Provide usable free judge access through the private submission instructions. The current owner credential is private and must not be committed or pasted publicly. No separate judge account or public sample-project access is claimed complete here.
- Recheck the submitted build, domain, setup instructions, supported-format statements and supplied links. The earlier instruction not to poll a deployment after pushing remains honored; documentation work has not certified the later CI/rollout result.
- Run/record actual Premiere import and relink before claiming Premiere acceptance. Run paired cleared-footage evaluation before claiming AI superiority. If still incomplete, retain the article's explicit limitations and show only the verified subset.
- Review eligibility, team/organization authorization, license/ownership and existing-work explanation. Choose Best Apps and Agents; a future hardware direction does not make the current app a robotics submission.
- Freeze the submitted commit and keep judging access available for the full judging period. Do not make restricted post-deadline changes without the organizer's permitted process.
