# Product

## Organize the shoot

Upload camera files directly to Cloud Storage. Choose automatic slate organization or a declared scene/shot/take. The interface distinguishes transfer progress, media processing and placement.

With the uploader's automatic policy enabled, an unambiguous slate can resolve or create scene/shot codes. Conflicting, missing and duplicate identities require review. Evidence and previous placements remain available; no footage is automatically deleted because of an AI judgement.

In-app navigation can retain an active browser transfer. Reload or browser closure may require reselecting the original files to resume. Server processing continues after arrival.

## Review the material

Open a scene, then a shot. The cockpit brings together take players, timecoded findings, comparison and human notes. Independent full-duration analysis does not require sibling takes or the comparative panel.

A finding can be confirmed, corrected, range-adjusted or dismissed. Dismissal removes it from the working view without erasing its history. An unanalysed take is not presented as fault-free.

Confirm multiple useful portions from one or several takes. Alternatives remain available even when a different take is preferred. A creative reason belongs to the editor; changing a performance preference is not automatically a technical model error.

## Preview the production

**Scene coverage** plays a scene's confirmed ranges in shot/range order.

**Film Preview** opens current confirmed portions across the project when no saved sequence exists. Missing selections are listed rather than silently declared complete. Current selects are a snapshot; refresh them to include later decisions.

**Saved sequence** is an independent ordered list of source portions. Arrange, trim within the source, add alternatives, repeat deliberately and save a revision. History can be loaded as a draft. Source recordings and shot selections are unchanged.

The browser preview uses hard cuts and source audio. It is not a rendered master, frame-accurate conform, colour grade or audio mix. Scene exports and the sequence JSON list support hand-off; JSON is not advertised as universal NLE interchange.

## Team workflow

People with project access use the same review workflow. Backend authority controls upload, curation and destructive actions; ownership still matters. Anonymous visitors can inspect public productions, not mutate them.

## Live measurements

Accuracy & review reports processed footage, completed full-duration analysis, reviewed findings and first non-withdrawn review agreement. It shows denominators, project scope and refresh time. Retractions exclude mistaken human judgements without deleting history. No reviews means Not measured, not 100%. These figures do not measure undetected issues or artistic quality.

## Performance workspace

A source recording may hold multiple performance attempts. The shot workspace exposes candidate boundaries, evidence/interpretation, shortlist and director-choice states. Candidates can be ordered by the fraction of flagged time; this is not a performance-quality rank. Compare across recordings or within one recording using up to four independently controlled viewers. Additional candidates remain available without starting more decoders.

Inspect footage and Compare performance attempts are modes of the same shot workspace. Group controls start, pause or resume selected viewers; per-viewer offsets align a chosen action/dialogue beat manually. This is browser comparison, not frame-locked synchronization or automatic beat matching. Analysis progress refreshes while a task is active without replacing a boundary draft.

Editors can split, merge, rename and adjust candidate ranges, undo/redo draft changes and restore saved revisions as a new revision. Adding a candidate to shot selects is explicit and retains its source/attempt revision. Existing shot and film ranges do not change when boundaries change. Source lanes show unexcluded time, not a guarantee that an entire recording is clean.
